About Us

The Booky Joint has been independent and locally owned since first opening in Mammoth Lakes in 1975. We moved to our current location in 1978 and have been serving the Eastern Sierra ever since. Nestled under beautiful Mammoth Mountain, we offer a large selection of new & used books, games, toys, maps, magazines, cards, puzzles, art supplies, Lego, and more.
